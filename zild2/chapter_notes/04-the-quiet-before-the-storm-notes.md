# Editorial Notes — Chapter 4 — The Quiet Before the Storm

## Dramatic function

This chapter assembles the crew's anxieties into an operational decision: the Ark's unexplained drift, the accelerating duct-beat, ghost power arrays, and a quarantined Mirrorglass message push the team from worried observation into action. It develops character through small domestic moments—Isaac's insomnia, Lauren's steadiness, Lena's obsession, Ava's reverence for plant response, Markus's readiness—while advancing several long arcs: Spacy's private secrecy, the Mirrorglass signal, and the ghost arrays tied to Dr. Eamon Shale. The chapter ends with a definitive choice to engage the Mirrorglass trajectory, raising stakes and carrying the plot into a zone of greater unknown.
