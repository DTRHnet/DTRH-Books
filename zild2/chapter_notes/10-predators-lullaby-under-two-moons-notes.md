# Editorial Notes — Chapter 10 — Predators Lullaby Under Two Moons

## Dramatic function

This chapter escalates the immediate threat: adaptive spores compromise the Ark’s food supply and force a strategic relocation to higher ground. It deepens character stakes—Lena’s exhaustion and secret discovery, Lauren and Markus’s command conflict, Ava’s frontline pragmatism—and opens two longer arcs: an unexplained encrypted file (ARK-SHADOW-10) that hints at hidden processes or agendas, and a subtle system irregularity (the duct-beat) that suggests the Ark or Spacy may be acting beyond human oversight. The chapter ends with the team descending toward the caverns (the Serpent’s Chorus), which shifts the action from surface containment to subterranean investigation and primes the Loom/inhibitor mysteries for payoff.
