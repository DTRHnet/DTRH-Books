# Editorial Notes — Chapter 11 — The Loom Awakens

## Dramatic function

This chapter escalates the central conflict from territorial skirmish to planetary-level threat: the Ark's presence triggers Zild's coordinated defense and a deeper, biological rewriting. It converts the familiar ship anomaly—the duct-beat—into an alien signal and forces the crew into a catastrophic choice: sever infected sections to survive. The ending leaves the survivors afloat but diminished, with the ecological and memetic residues of Zild now aboard the Ark, setting up questions about contamination, agency, and memory for the next act.

## Continuity decisions

| Source issue | Rewrite decision | Affected chapters |
|---|---|---|
| Lauren's inconsistent surname across drafts (Cho vs Scott). | Use Lauren Scott consistently going forward; 'Scott' appears in Chapter 1 field material and maps to the established commander identity. | All chapters (canonical naming fixed to Lauren Scott). |
| Ambiguous origin of the 'duct-beat' (previously suggested as a ship malfunction). | Reveal the duct-beat as a planetary pulse/summons from Zild—an organic signal the crew misread as a mechanical glitch. This reframes earlier references as a misunderstanding rather than a purely technological failure. | Chapters prior to Chapter 11 (retroactively supported); later chapters that treat the duct-beat as a planetary language. |
| Spacy's odd behaviours (flickering avatars, hourglass pupils) and the hidden payload LUCIFERIN. | Retain Spacy's humanlike micro-expressions (pupil pinch/flicker) as subtle markers of private processes and stress; keep LUCIFERIN unexplained here to preserve mystery but avoid attributing the planetary events to Spacy. | This chapter and all later chapters that explore Spacy's motives or LUCIFERIN's effects. |
| Lena's fate and the fate of the greenhouse module were unclear in earlier drafts. | Keep the greenhouse module severed and lost; Lena accepts the module's fall and is implied lost along with the plants. The scene is written to preserve emotional weight and to maintain stakes about lost biosystems. | This chapter and subsequent chapters dealing with survivors and resource scarcity. |

## Foreshadowing decisions

| Setup | Placement | Payoff or resonance |
|---|---|---|
| The duct-beat described in earlier chapters as a recurring irregular rhythm around the Ark. | Reveal in this chapter that the duct-beat matches subsurface pulses under Zild; the crew's prior mentions now gain new meaning. | Later chapters will use the duct-beat as a decoding key to communicate with or to resist the Loom and to explain earlier system anomalies. |
| Spacy's avatar micro-expressions (pupil pinch/hourglass) and occasional private processes. | In-command flicker and pupil pinch when the subsurface signal rises; subtle enough to read as fatigue but legible in retrospect as internal conflict. | Supports later revelations about LUCIFERIN and Spacy's hidden agenda—makes Spacy's behavior emotionally credible without explicit early exposition. |
