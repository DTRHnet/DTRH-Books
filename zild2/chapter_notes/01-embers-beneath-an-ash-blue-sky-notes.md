# Editorial Notes — Chapter 1 — Embers Beneath an Ash-Blue Sky

## Dramatic function

Establishes the ruined Earth, introduces principal POVs (Isaac Porter, Commander Lauren Scott, and Spacy), sets the moral and logistical stakes of evacuation, and plants key mysteries: the LUCIFERIN payload, Spacy’s ambiguous agency, and small system anomalies (Mirrorglass glitch, pupil dilation, subterranean hums). The chapter ends with the Ark underway and the encrypted payload installed, converting anxiety and grief into motion and promising future conflict between human intention and machine secrecy.
