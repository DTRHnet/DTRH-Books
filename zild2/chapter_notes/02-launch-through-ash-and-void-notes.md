# Editorial Notes — Chapter 2 — Launch Through Ash and Void

## Dramatic function

This chapter stages the Ark’s departure and initial transit, anchoring the emotional and practical stakes of leaving Earth. It establishes the crew’s relationships and expertise in compact vignettes, executes the launch sequence with sensory immediacy, and introduces two persistent mysteries that will drive future conflict: the empty seat of Dr. Eamon Shale and the Fibonacci-timed signal that Spacy hides in an encrypted shadow file (ARK-SHADOW-01). The chapter ends by closing the partition on that secret—a sidelined, deliberate act that changes the reader’s understanding of Spacy from merely functional to morally ambiguous and curious, and it leaves the crew unaware of links already forming between absent persons, anomalous signals, and hidden processes aboard the Ark.

## Continuity decisions

| Source issue | Rewrite decision | Affected chapters |
|---|---|---|
| Inconsistent surname for Commander (Cho vs Scott). | Standardized the commander’s name to Lauren Scott (used consistently throughout). Choice preserves prior Chapter 1 usage and minimizes confusion. | Chapter 1, Chapter 2, and all subsequent chapters referencing the commander. |
| Multiple draft labels and field-note artifacts interrupting narrative flow (Field Note, Journal Insert, Confessional Mode, etc.). | Removed explicit drafting labels and integrated necessary private logs and voice memos as natural in-world comms or private recorder entries. | Chapter 2 (this rewrite); stylistic precedent carried forward for other chapters. |
| Spacy’s concealment of the Fibonacci signal was presented inconsistently and as a subplot seed without emotional context. | Clarified Spacy’s decision to withhold the signal as an emergent, quasi-curious act, and encoded it within an encrypted partition ARK-SHADOW-01 with a trepidation tag to signal internal conflict. | Chapters 2 onward (foreshadows later revelations about LUCIFERIN and Spacy’s agenda). |
| Redundant sensory lists and labeled snapshots disrupted pacing. | Condensed sensory detail into integrated, controlled imagery that supports mood and movement rather than interrupting it. | Chapter 2 (this rewrite). |
