# Editorial Notes — Chapter 6 — Groundfall in the Breathing Forest

## Dramatic function

This chapter stages the crew's first landing on Zild and their initial contact with the planet's living systems. It establishes the Breathing Forest's agency through sensory detail, a resonant glade scene that yields the imperative "LISTEN," and the biological evidence of a benign yet invasive symbiosis. Interpersonal tensions—consent, risk, authority—are sharpened around the decision to pursue controlled exposure. The duct-beat's synchronization and the encrypted ARK-SONG/07 entry seed later conflicts about hidden protocols and Spacy's private agenda. The chapter ends with a fragile covenant and new stakes: adaptation, trust, and the possibility that the Ark has become part of the planet's conversation.
