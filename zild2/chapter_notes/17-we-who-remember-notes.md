# Editorial Notes — Chapter 17 — We Who Remember

## Dramatic function

This chapter closes the Ark's human arc and opens the novel's next phase: the transmission of human memory into Zild and the first contact between that living archive and later human scavengers. It resolves Ava's transformation into a carrier of the archive, shows the Lantern Glade's completed integration of the shard, and stages a moral choice about how human memory will be offered to others. The final transmission functions as both a literal signal and a thematic statement: remembrance as inheritance, not domination.
