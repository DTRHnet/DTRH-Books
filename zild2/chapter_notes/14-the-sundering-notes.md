# Editorial Notes — Chapter 14 — The Sundering

## Dramatic function

This chapter executes the Ark's catastrophic Sundering: the infection of sector twelve, the horrific emergence of ship/planet hybrids, and the consequential sacrifice of sector twelve to save the remainder of the crew. It closes one axis of the rescue narrative by removing Isaac and Markus from the active roster, sharpens the moral stakes for Lauren Scott (choice, guilt, command), and pivots the survivors toward a fraught strategy: negotiation via biological integration or a scorched-earth refusal. The ending propels the crew into the reactor/core—an interior threshold leading directly to the next confrontation—and converts earlier system anomalies (duct-beat, spiral glyph) into immediate, actionable threats and possible keys.
