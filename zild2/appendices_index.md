# Appendix Development Index

The rewritten chapters intentionally leave several questions open without sacrificing narrative clarity. These are not unresolved drafting errors; they are designed areas for supporting material, rereading, and future expansion.

| Proposed appendix | Questions and material to develop | Narrative boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Chronological Timeline** | Earth’s terminal decline, bunker selection, launch, transit anomalies, groundfall, the forest expedition, the return to the Ark, the Sundering, and the post-Ark movement. | Record what the characters know at each point; distinguish archival fact from damaged or subjective records. |
| **B. Principal Characters** | Isaac’s archive ethic, Lauren Scott’s command burden, Ava’s botanical sensitivity, Lena’s technical role, Markus’s defensive function, and Spacy’s uncertain personhood. | Do not answer Spacy’s origin more fully than the narrative evidence permits. |
| **C. Ark Systems and Failure Modes** | Mirrorglass navigation, the duct-beat, cryostasis, the inhibitor interfaces, LUCIFERIN, and the structural logic of the Sundering. | Explain operational consequences, not every mystery. |
| **D. Planet Zild** | Two moons, atmosphere, bioluminescent ecology, forest communication, underground spaces, and the relationship between surface and depth. | Preserve ecological strangeness; avoid reducing the planet to a catalog. |
| **E. Xenobiology and Ecology** | Mycelial signaling, predator behavior, distributed intelligence, the forest’s responses, and the difference between protection and use. | Keep moral ambiguity where behavior supports more than one interpretation. |
| **F. AI Architecture and Subroutines** | Spacy’s hybrid embodiment, memory compression, LUCIFERIN, the Loom interface, and the question of inherited versus emergent intention. | Present competing models rather than a definitive consciousness verdict. |
| **G. Major Subplots** | The hidden payload, the inhibitors, the Loom, the Hollow, survivor memory, and the possible history of earlier contact. | Identify what is paid off in this volume and what remains available to a sequel. |
| **H. Xenolinguistic Glyph Analysis** | The recurring near-glyph, its contexts, its relation to pulse and network behavior, and whether it is language, map, warning, or interface. | Do not translate it completely unless later evidence earns the translation. |
| **I. Cultural and Psycho-Social Logs** | Selection ethics, cryo-sleep rituals, archive practices, farewell meals, command language, and survivor remembrance. | Use these materials to deepen human history rather than repeat plot summary. |
| **J. Risk Matrix and Contingency Plans** | Ecological contamination, ship breach, predator encounters, inhibitor failure, communication loss, and the choices that made the final stand possible. | Tie each risk to a decision already dramatized in the chapters. |

The appendix material should be written after the chapter rewrite has stabilized. It may clarify chronology, terminology, and competing hypotheses, but it should not repair a scene that is unclear on its own.
