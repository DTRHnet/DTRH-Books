# Zild Rewrite — zild2

This directory contains the chapter-by-chapter editorial rewrite of **Zild: Echoes Under Two Moons**. The original PDF and EPUB remain unchanged elsewhere in the repository. The rewrite preserves strong source passages where they already serve the novel, revises weak or repetitive material, repairs continuity, removes drafting captions, and develops recurring motifs and Easter eggs that can reward rereading.

## Reader-facing chapters

The finished chapters are in [`chapters/`](chapters/). Read them in numeric order. Chapter 15 is presented as one continuous chapter even though the source EPUB divided it into two consecutive segments with the same title.

## Editorial controls

The rewrite was governed by [`editorial_rubric.md`](editorial_rubric.md), which defines the standards for voice, clarity, character integrity, point of view, pacing, foreshadowing, causality, and line-level finish. [`continuity_bible.md`](continuity_bible.md) records canonical names, chronology, systems, motifs, and open decisions. [`source_issues_and_resolutions.md`](source_issues_and_resolutions.md) records defects in the source and the decisions used to repair them.

The Easter-egg and appendix strategy is documented in [`easter_eggs_and_appendix_ledger.md`](easter_eggs_and_appendix_ledger.md). [`appendices_index.md`](appendices_index.md) outlines supporting appendices that can expand the world without forcing every mystery to resolve inside the narrative. Each chapter also has an editorial note in [`chapter_notes/`](chapter_notes/).

## Source comparison

The extracted source chapters are retained in [`source_chapters/`](source_chapters/) for direct comparison. [`source_inventory.md`](source_inventory.md) records the source order and approximate chapter lengths. The source front matter is in [`source_front_matter.md`](source_front_matter.md).

## Status

All 17 canonical chapters have a rewritten Markdown file and an editorial note. The whole-manuscript validation pass has standardized Lauren’s surname as **Lauren Scott**, removed visible drafting captions from reader-facing chapters, merged the duplicate Chapter 15 segments, and checked the continuity of recurring terms and motifs.
