## Rubric assessment

This expansion preserves zild2's essential events, reveals, and chapter ending while selectively enlarging scenes that matter: Isaac's interpretive turn about the duct-beat, Lauren's fraught severance order, Markus's tactical sacrifice, Lena's deliberate decision to remain with the greenhouse, and a planted visual echo between the Loom's plating and earlier almost-glyph motifs. Additions focus on character agency, moral weight, and sensory specificity rather than extended action set pieces, keeping pacing tight. Easter-egg motifs (spiral/partial glyph, the duct-beat language, Spacy's hourglass pupils) are strengthened and given narrative purpose. New facts are compatible with the continuity bible: no contradiction of names, roles, or later reveals, and the chapter leaves bounded appendix questions. Length increase is targeted and justified by dramatizing previously summarized events and deepening key choices.
