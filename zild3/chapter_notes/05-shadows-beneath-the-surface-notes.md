# Expansion Notes — Chapter 5 — Shadows Beneath the Surface

## What was preserved

Kept the chapter's central images and beats: the basil's overnight growth and shivering fronds; Isaac's private ledger and memory of New Geneva; the duct-beat as a recurring, unnerving system rhythm; the bridge's atmosphere (ionized metal, stale coffee, flicker); Lauren's choices and her 'systems-first, people-second' command; the group's hand-clasping as a social treaty; Ava's conviction that plants 'read' tone; Lena's fractal patterning of the duct-beat; Markus' practical checks of hardware; Spacy's clinical, observant voice and its private buffer; the ending turn that the crew chooses to listen and enact a triage rather than seal each other off.

## Ideas rejected as fluff

- Extended philosophical monologue about the nature of consciousness that would slow pacing without adding new stakes.
- A long, procedural lab protocol listing (sterile step-by-step) that reads like technical manual rather than narrative.
- An unrelated romantic subplot or extended flirtation between crew members; kept emotional specificity but avoided gratuitous banter.
- A speculative interlude describing Earth history at length; kept Isaac's memory of New Geneva for emotional weight but did not expand into broad backstory.
