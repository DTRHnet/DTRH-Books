## Rubric assessment

This expansion preserves the chapter's existing emotional trajectory and core reveals while deepening character agency, procedures, and archival behavior. Key passages and motif frequencies (duct-beat intervals, teal motes, ARK-SHADOW logs, Shale's prime-number interest) are retained and given more weight. New material dramatizes the quarantine and clandestine pact, gives Dr. Shale and Markus concrete actions that can reverberate later, and makes Spacy's private archival choices explicit—planting necessary seeds for the LUCIFERIN and archive reveals. Additions avoid introducing new, unsupported plotlines and instead create bounded evidence trails (almost-glyph, fractured lens, ARK-SHADOW files) that can be followed in later chapters or appendices. The pacing alternates observation, procedural action, and personal choice; the ending turn (duct-beat sliding to thirty-six seconds and the Ark continuing into the 'serpent's coil') is preserved and strengthened by the added stakes.
