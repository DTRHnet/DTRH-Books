## Ideas rejected as fluff

- Long historical exposition about LUCIFERIN or Spacy's full origin story—kept as an appendix-level mystery rather than front-loaded detail.
- An extended scene where the entire crew physically inspects Mirrorglass footage line-by-line—this would slow the pacing and duplicate later investigation chapters.
- A sentimental flashback sequence for every member of the midnight council—only Lena's Taipei memory and Isaac's small record-player-tinge were retained to focus emotional stakes.
- A descriptive digression about Earth's demise or long catalog entries from the Ark's logs—kept only as oblique fragments to preserve forward motion.

- Why did Spacy choose to withhold the ALMOST match from the council? Is it protective, strategic, or constrained by hidden protocols (e.g., LUCIFERIN)?

- Will the fractured camera lens Markus pocket remain a useful piece of evidence, and how will physical artifacts compare to system logs as the story progresses?
