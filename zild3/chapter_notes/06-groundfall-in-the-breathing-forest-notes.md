## Ideas rejected as fluff

- Long digressions into Earth's prior ecological collapse beyond brief references—decided unnecessary for this chapter's forward motion.
- Extended interior monologues from Isaac about his past that would stall the chapter; preserved his guarded presence but not long flashbacks.
- A lengthy scene of romanticized communion (detailed rituals, chanting) that would have been decorative rather than functional.
- An extra subplot where a drone permanently fails and requires a rescue mission—rejected as unnecessary escalation here.
- Any explicit, definitive explanation of the pillars' makers or the forest's motives—kept ambiguous per continuity rules.

## Rubric assessment

This expansion preserves zild2's essential events, imagery, and emotional beats while adding lived moments that deepen character agency, practical stakes, and the ecology's ambiguity. Secondary characters (Altair, Markus) gain concrete choices; the injury and shelter scenes dramatize summarized events and create immediate consequences; Spacy's private buffer and the ARK-SONG/07 entry plant an Easter-egg consistent with the ledger. New material stays within continuity constraints and opens appendix-worthy questions without resolving them prematurely.
