# Zild3 Expansion Rubric

## Expansion goal

Zild3 is an expanded edition of zild2, not a replacement of its story. Every chapter should become fuller through meaningful additions: scene texture, character interaction, practical obstacles, emotional consequence, world detail, and carefully placed Easter eggs. Expansion may approach twice the zild2 length but must not exceed twice the chapter’s zild2 word count unless a structural reason is documented.

## What may be expanded

A chapter may gain material when the addition does at least one of the following: clarifies a character’s choice, gives a secondary character agency, dramatizes a consequence that zild2 summarized, makes a location physically legible, deepens the ecology or technology through action, strengthens a transition between chapters, plants a later Easter egg, or opens a question suitable for an appendix.

The expansion should not become padding. New paragraphs must earn their place through conflict, discovery, relationship, atmosphere with narrative function, or thematic development.

## Chapter expansion standards

| Category | zild3 standard |
|---|---|
| Preservation | Keep zild2’s essential events, decisions, reveals, chapter order, canonical names, and emotional trajectory. |
| Selective enlargement | Preserve effective zild2 prose where it works; expand around it rather than mechanically paraphrasing every sentence. |
| Scene depth | Convert selected summaries into lived moments with setting, action, sensory detail, dialogue, and consequence. |
| Character agency | Give Isaac, Lauren Scott, Lena, Ava, Markus, Spacy, and relevant secondary figures distinct choices or reactions where the source permits. |
| Continuity | Do not contradict the zild2 continuity bible. Any new fact must be compatible with later chapters or be logged as an open question. |
| Pacing | Alternate pressure, discovery, reflection, and recovery. Added material must not delay every turn or flatten escalation. |
| Easter eggs | Add subtle recurring details—objects, phrases, glyphs, sounds, scents, or behavioral patterns—that gain meaning later. Never label them as Easter eggs. |
| Appendix openings | Leave intriguing but bounded questions for appendices. The chapter itself must remain understandable without appendix material. |
| Voice | Match Chapter One and zild2: atmospheric, intelligent, emotionally grounded, and specific without ornamental overload. |
| Draft-artifact removal | Do not restore labels such as “Planting Seed,” “Sensory Snapshot,” or “Journal Insert” as process captions. Any document form must be dramatically motivated. |
| Ending integrity | Preserve each chapter’s major ending turn while giving it more preparation, emotional weight, or resonance. |

## Continuity safeguards

The zild2 folder is read-only reference material for this pass. Zild3 must use the canonical Lauren Scott naming, one Chapter 15, the established duct-beat/LUCIFERIN/Loom/inhibitor sequence, and the existing ending’s core direction. New material may complicate interpretation but may not erase a payoff already earned in zild2.

Each chapter receives an expansion note with four parts: what was preserved, what was added, which Easter eggs were planted or strengthened, and which appendix questions remain open. A whole-manuscript ledger will record new facts so later chapters can use them consistently.

## Length rule

The target range is approximately **1.25x to 2.0x** each zild2 chapter’s word count. Chapters may remain closer to zild2 when their existing pacing is already strong, but no chapter should be expanded merely to meet a number. The final validation will report chapter-by-chapter ratios and flag any chapter below 1.15x or above 2.0x for review.
